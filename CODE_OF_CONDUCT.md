# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SideSeat to be a project where anyone can report a bug, ask a naive question, or disagree with a
design decision without it costing them anything socially. That is the whole of it.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is trying to help, and say what you mean plainly.
- Critique the code, the design, or the argument — not the person.
- When you are shown to be wrong, say so and move on. Nobody is keeping score.
- Respect that maintainers' time is finite: an unanswered issue is a queue, not a snub.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning language relating to age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
  gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance,
  race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing a review.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers at
[github.com/sideseat/sideseat/security/advisories/new](https://github.com/sideseat/sideseat/security/advisories/new)
(a private channel; mark it clearly as a conduct report, not a vulnerability) or by direct message to a
maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow this in good faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will decide, and explain, the response to any behaviour they judge inappropriate: a private word,
an edited or removed comment, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. The reasoning will be given to the person
concerned.
